Sukoon · Design system

Motion specification

How Sukoon moves — and, more often, how it holds still. Every animation in the app: its trigger, timing, easing and reduced-motion fallback, each with a live demo you can replay. Timings are named tokens so a build reproduces them exactly.

v1.0 · 16 Jul 2026· 12 motions· 6 duration + 4 easing tokens· use the Tweaks panel to slow the demos down
Principles
01
Stillness is the default
Motion is the exception, not the texture. Most state changes are a near-instant colour or opacity shift. Travel is spent only on real spatial change — navigation, sheets, alerts.
02
One calm curve
A single signature easing — --e-calm — carries the emotional moments. Utility moves use the standard curve. Nothing bounces or springs.
03
Slow to arrive, quick to leave
Entrances decelerate and take their time so a reading feels considered. Exits accelerate and get out of the way. Enter with --e-out, leave with --e-in.
04
Loops are rationed
Only genuinely live or urgent states repeat forever — the live dot, an active scan, an unacknowledged low. Everything else settles once and stops.

Reduced motion
Honour the system setting, always

Under prefers-reduced-motion, six rules replace every animation above. Nothing disappears — information still arrives, just without travel.

1Ambient loops (pulse, ripple) freeze at a legible steady state — no breathing.
2Spatial transitions (push, sheet, banner, toast) become a 120ms opacity crossfade in place.
3Data reveals (ring, count-up, graph) render their final value at once, with one short fade.
4Controls change state instantly; only colour crossfades, capped at 90ms.
5Staggered lists appear together — no per-item delay or offset.
6The urgent-low takeover appears immediately; colour and copy carry the alarm, the reading does not pulse.
