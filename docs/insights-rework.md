# Insights, reworked: less data, one thing to do

Kai, 2026-10-12: Insights "bombards me with data without giving me any action". Up to 21 equal cards,
mostly numbers, most ending in "discuss with your care team"; lows told five times over.

## The screen (Trends → Insights), top to bottom

1. **Your 14 days**: one slim line: time in range and its arrow against last week ("78 %, ↑ 4 since
   last week"). Tap: the full targets table.
2. **The one thing**: the most important finding this fortnight, in one sentence, with one line of
   evidence and one button. Priority: lows (urgent first) → nights → a hard meal time → variability →
   habits (injection timing, sites, finger-pricks). Only one.
3. **Also worth knowing**: at most 3 short cards, one sentence each; tap to open the numbers behind it.
4. **Everything else**: the remaining findings, folded under one row ("6 more findings").

Not enough data: one card that says what to log to unlock it ("Log meals for 5 days and I can tell you
which meal time is hardest"), with the button that logs one.

## Actions: only ones that would change something

Each finding has a list of actions in order; the first one **not already in effect** is the button.
When none applies, the button is **Look at it** (opens the graph on the days/hours it's about).

| Finding | Actions, in order (skipped when already done) |
|---|---|
| Lows at a certain time / at night | Turn on *Going low* · Look at those nights · Report for your doctor (finding highlighted) |
| Lows after workouts | Turn on the after-workout check · Look at them |
| Doses close together before lows | Turn on dose suggestions (they count active insulin) · Look at them |
| A hard meal time | Open that meal's ratio in Learning (suggestions on) · Log insulin with that meal (so it can learn) · Look at it |
| Sensor vs finger-prick apart | Turn on calibration · Check the sensor |
| Same injection spot | Open the site map |
| Dawn rise, variability, rich meals | Look at it · Report for your doctor |

Actions only switch app settings or open screens. Insulin numbers still change only through
Learning's *Use* (at most 20 % at a time).

## The data

- **Merged:** recurring lows, nights with lows, lows after workouts, stacking and rebounds become one
  *Your lows* finding with its likely cause (the strongest one names the action).
- **Removed:** the 500/1800 formulas card (it's dose setup, not a finding; it stays there).
- **Confidence:** every finding says what it's from ("4 of 14 nights", "from 6 lunches") and has a
  minimum; below it, nothing is claimed.
- **Window:** 14 days, the same as the Report's default; warm-up readings never count.

## New: after-workout check

When a workout is logged (yours or from MyFitnessPal), 30 minutes after it ends: a notification with
your number and arrow ("After your workout: 92 ↘"), tap opens Now. Lows still have their own alarms;
this is the gentle look before one. On by default; off in You → Alarms.
